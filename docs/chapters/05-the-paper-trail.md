# Chapter 5: The Paper Trail

Region: `cities`. Office hours. This is the stealth chapter. Introduces agents, Act Natural and Lock-in. Pruitt's umbrella is the weapon.

## The bar

The bar of the hotel across the street from the records office, at ten in the morning (`c5.bar`). A barman polishing one glass. Coffee that has been on since six, and one tea bag that is kept for guests.

## Beats

1. **The hotel bar** (`c5.explore.bar`). The hot drink and the kit.
2. **The lobby** (`c5.explore.lobby`). The Office of Atmospheric Records takes enquiries by ticket. Andrew's number is 847. They are serving 12. Colour clue: `c5.clue.retirement`, a noticeboard with a retirement party sign-up sheet for an Agent Pruitt, three years out.
3. **Act Natural.** Myko observes that nobody has ever looked at a man in a queue. Act Natural is added to the kit as a move for slot 1: standing still beside a marked prop, Andrew cannot be seen by guards until he moves. Taught on the lobby guard, to reach the Staff door.
4. **The records floor** (`c5.stealth.floor`, stealth). Six `agent`s patrol in overlapping loops. Props: two vending machines, the water cooler, a noticeboard, a queue for the photocopier. Louder footsteps at Bit much matter here more than anywhere. Clean-run reward: `c5.clue.memo`, a memo from Pruitt asking, for the fourth time, for a second stapler.
5. **Files** (`c5.explore.files`). Key clues: `c5.clue.requisition` (a requisition for "replacement corn, 40 acres"), `c5.clue.busticket` (an expenses claim for a bus to somewhere redacted). Five loose sheets from one redacted file are scattered through the cubicles.
6. **Redaction puzzle** (`c5.puzzle.redacted`, puzzle). At the lightbox, choose three of the five sheets to overlay. Each copy has different words blacked out, and the right three together show one full sentence. A wrong set shows a different, useless sentence. Reward: key clue `c5.clue.site`, naming a test plot in Wendell County, Ohio.
7. **The photocopier.** Andrew copies the page for the casebook. The copier jams, and the jam alarm is the building alarm.
8. **Lock-in.** As the agents close in, Myko asks how Dympna keeps a room when she wants it kept. Lock-in goes into slot 3: enemies near Andrew are held for 4 seconds and take double melee damage. It needs Bit much; the break-room fridge has a birthday whiskey for anyone not there yet.
9. **Cubicles** (`c5.fight.floor`, fight). Agents in numbers: three waves of three `agent`s, coming through different cubicle lanes. The first fight where flanking matters.
10. **Deduction board** (`c5.deduction.ohio`, deduction). Link `c5.clue.site`, `c5.clue.requisition` and `c5.clue.busticket` to name the lead: Wendell County, Ohio.
11. **Agent Pruitt, round one** (`c5.boss.pruitt1`, boss).

## Boss: Agent Pruitt, round one

Tired and courteous. He asks Andrew to stop, once, and means it kindly. He speaks to Andrew alone.

- **Phase 1.** He stays at range. Taser dart (ordinary), and a hand crank that slides a rolling shelf to close the lane Andrew is in (floor rails light up 1 second before). Being caught between shelves is a heavy hit and pushes Andrew out.
- **Phase 2 (half health).** Two `agent`s join. Pruitt cranks two shelves at once, leaving one safe lane.
- **End.** It is five o'clock. Pruitt stops, puts on his coat and says he will have to write this up in the morning. The exit is unlocked. He tells Andrew to take the umbrella from the stand.

Arena: 30 × 16 of the stacks, five lanes.

## Back to the bar

Across the street. Once the records floor is passed its agents are gone and the Staff door stays open.
