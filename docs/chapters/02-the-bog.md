# Chapter 2: The Bog

Region: `kilnamoe`. Before dawn, mist. Introduces the casebook, stealth, Reflex Test and The Stumble.

## The bar

A lean-to at the edge of the bog, beside the gate on `c2.bog`: a crate of stout, two chairs, an honesty box and a flask. The turf cutters keep it. Nobody is there. The flask has tea in it and there is a jar of coffee beside it. Andrew has nothing to put in the box.

## Beats

1. **The lean-to** (`c2.explore.bar`). The hot drink and the kit.
2. **The ring** (`c2.explore.ring`). Casebook tutorial. Key clues: `c2.clue.ring` (scorched turf, a perfect circle, 9 metres), `c2.clue.fragment` (metal, warm, stamped MAS-4471-K), `c2.clue.tracks` (twin-wheel van tracks leaving towards Brennan's). Colour clues: `c2.clue.frog`, a boiled frog; `c2.clue.welly`, a single burnt welly.
3. **The Department men.** A van arrives. Three men in suits get out and say they are from the Department of Agriculture to nobody in particular. Myko, by phone from the gate, reminds Andrew that a thrown bottle makes a noise where it lands and that these men will go and look.
4. **The bog crossing** (`c2.stealth.bog`, stealth). Three `suit`s with torches sweep the bog. Turf stacks break line of sight. Follow the tyre tracks to the farm. Clean-run reward: `c2.clue.lanyard`, a dropped visitor pass reading Meridian Agri-Solutions.
5. **The yard** (`c2.puzzle.sheep`, puzzle). The tracks end at the hay shed and the yard is full of sheep. Three gates each swing between two positions. Sheep move away from Andrew and settle in whichever pen is open. Get the flock into the far pen to clear the shed door. Solvable in five gate changes.
6. **The shed** (`c2.explore.shed`). Key clue: `c2.clue.docket`, a delivery docket for "soil harmoniser, 40 units" from the Meridian depot, signed for by nobody. Pickup: the Brennans' good whiskey, behind the hay. Myko says what it will do and does not expect to be listened to.
7. **The Stumble.** The first time Andrew is at Bit much after the shed, he goes through a stack of feed buckets by accident. Myko suggests doing it on purpose. Key 3 lurches in a straight line through everything in the way. The move goes into slot 3. If the player never drinks the whiskey, the lesson waits for the first time Andrew reaches Bit much, in any chapter.
8. **Two cousins** (`c2.fight.yard`, fight). Two `cousin`s have heard about last night and one `lad` has come for the look of it. As the first one squares up, Myko asks Andrew where a man folds. Reflex Test is added to the kit as a second move for slot 1.
9. **Casebook conclusion.** No deduction board yet. Andrew writes the conclusion himself (`c2.clue.conclusion`: the fragment, the tracks and the docket all point to the depot) and Myko's margin note agrees for the first time, with reservations.
10. **The ram** (`c2.setpiece.ram`, setpiece).

## Set piece: the ram

A Brennan ram takes exception to Andrew at the yard gate. It cannot be hurt.

- Andrew runs the length of `c2.lane`, left to right, with the ram behind. The camera leads.
- Obstacles: puddles (slow), a wheelbarrow, a gate that must be opened with E, a sleeping dog (go round).
- A thrown bottle that lands near the ram makes it stop and look for 1.5 seconds.
- A butt from the ram is a heavy hit and knocks Andrew forward, which is not entirely unhelpful.
- It ends at the cattle grid, which the ram will not cross. It stands there and looks at him.
- Myko has been sitting on the first gate the whole time.
- If Andrew ends up sober or on the floor during the run he is back at the lean-to and the run starts again at the yard gate.

## Back to the bar

The lean-to is at the bog gate. Once the bog crossing is passed the suits are gone, and the walk to the farm is the tyre tracks. The sheep stay penned.

## Sections for call-in

`c2.puzzle.sheep`, `c2.fight.yard`, `c2.setpiece.ram` (after the blackout the ram is walking home; Myko says he knows the animal).

## Closing scene

At the cattle grid. Andrew lays out the case. Myko says a depot on the edge of town that sells nothing is at least odd, and that is as far as he will go.
