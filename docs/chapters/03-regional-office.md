# Chapter 3: Regional Office

Region: `kilnamoe`. Night, sodium yard lights. Introduces the deduction board, Same Again and One for the Road. Halloran's clipboard is the weapon.

## The bar

The depot's staff social club: the portacabin just inside the gap behind the skip on `c3.yard`. Its door faces away from the yard and the watchman's loop does not come round that side. Inside: a dartboard, a fridge, a kettle and a sign-up sheet for a Christmas party with no names on it. Nobody has ever used the room.

## Beats

1. **The social club** (`c3.explore.bar`). The hot drink and the kit.
2. **The yard** (`c3.stealth.yard`, stealth). One night watchman (a `suit` who walks a fixed loop and stops to check his phone) and two more `suit`s by the vans. A thrown bottle is the tool. Clean-run reward: `c3.clue.logbook`, the watchman's log, nine years of entries that all say "Nothing".
3. **The office** (`c3.explore.office`). Key clues: `c3.clue.map` (a wall map with one pin in Leitrim and one in Boston, and string between them), `c3.clue.brochure` (a product range with no prices and no descriptions). Colour clues: `c3.clue.certificate`, Halloran's "Regional Manager of the Year" certificate, awarded by Halloran; `c3.clue.visitors`, a visitors' book with no visitors.
4. **Same Again.** Halloran's filing cabinet is locked. One hit dents it. Myko says to hit it the same again before it has finished being hit. Same Again is added to the kit as a second move for slot 2; with it on, the cabinet opens. It holds a whiskey, filed under W.
5. **Crate puzzle** (`c3.puzzle.crates`, puzzle). The manifest is in the bay office and the door is behind stacked crates. Push-only crates on an 8 × 6 grid, five crates, two fixed pillars. A reset lever sits by the entrance. Solvable in nine pushes.
6. **The manifest** (`c3.explore.bayoffice`). Key clue: `c3.clue.manifest` (forty containers a year to Pier Nine Freight, Boston; contents listed as "agricultural"). The lights come on.
7. **Night shift** (`c3.fight.bay`, fight). Four `worker`s throw boxes from the gantry side while two `lad`s in hi-vis close in. The lesson is range: bottles reach the gantry, and the boxes land where their shadows say.
8. **First deduction board** (`c3.deduction.boston`, deduction). Link `c3.clue.map`, `c3.clue.manifest` and `c2.clue.fragment` (the part number prefix matches the container prefix) to name the lead: Pier Nine Freight, Boston. The board tutorial is Myko explaining that this is just string and he has seen Andrew do it on the kitchen wall.
9. **One for the Road.** Before the bay doors, Myko tells Andrew about the one punch he has in him when he is as far gone as he can go. It goes into slot 4, which only works at Fecked. The lesson is information: whether to go up there is the player's choice. A crate beside the doors holds two stouts for anyone who wants to try.
10. **Mr Halloran** (`c3.boss.halloran`, boss).

## Boss: Mr Halloran and his forklift

He introduces himself as regional manager, mentions the region is this building, and gets on the forklift.

- **Phase 1.** Fork charge in a straight line with a reversing-beep telegraph before each run (a heavy hit). If the forks hit a crate stack they stick for 2.5 seconds and he can be hit from the side. He also lifts and drops a pallet as an area attack with a shadow marker (heavy).
- **Phase 2 (half health).** He carries a pallet on the forks as a shield. Andrew has to step out of the charge and hit him from behind. Charges come in pairs.
- **End.** The forklift runs out of gas. Halloran stays seated, asks whether Andrew has an appointment, and hands him the clipboard to sign in. He says to keep it; he has another.

Arena: 26 × 14 of the loading bay, four crate stacks that break after two hits each.

## Back to the bar

The portacabin is by the gap. With the yard passed and the crate puzzle solved, the walk to the bay is the office and the cleared grid.

## Closing scene

Outside the depot. Andrew says Boston as though it settles things, and it does not, because he has €14. Myko says the flights are booked and will not discuss where the money came from. Nobody sees a ticket.
