# Chapter 7: Gift Shop

Region: `roswell`. Midday glare. Introduces aliens in disguise, tequila and Last Orders. The inflatable alien is the weapon. Everyone is dressed as an alien. Some of them are aliens.

From here on, about two in three of Myko's tangents are about Ohio. Roswell is compared with it and loses.

## The bar

An open-fronted themed bar at the bus-stop end of `c7.strip`. The bartender wears antennae and is sunburnt. The drinks are green. The first tequila is on the house. The hot drink is coffee or green tea; the tea is the one thing that is green on purpose.

## Beats

1. **The bar** (`c7.explore.bar`). The hot drink, the kit and the first tequila. Myko says what tequila does.
2. **The strip** (`c7.explore.strip`). Andrew has come four thousand miles to prove aliens exist and is now in a town where saying so is a business model. Nobody is interested. Colour clue: `c7.clue.leaflet`, a tourist leaflet with Andrew's bog ring on it, listed as "unverified, Europe".
3. **Who is an alien** (`c7.puzzle.tells`, puzzle). In the shop there are six figures in alien costumes. Three are tourists and three are aliens wearing alien costumes over themselves. Examine each for tells, then accuse three. The tells: no sweat in the heat, blinking sideways, standing in a perfect queue of one, counting change to the cent, reading the snow globes' small print. Tourists have human tells: sunburn, a phone, complaining. A wrong accusation embarrasses Andrew and costs nothing. Which three are aliens is fixed, not random, so the dialogue can be written to it.
4. **Gary.** The third alien is Gary, behind the till, who admits it at once. He has worked here for 19 years and likes it. He is the first person in the game to take Andrew seriously, and he is a bit bored by the subject. He gives key clue `c7.clue.payslip` (his payslip, issued by Meridian Basin Facility, Nevada). He talks to Andrew.
5. **Last Orders.** It is six o'clock. Gary says "Folks, we're closing" and every tourist leaves at once. He has done it for nineteen years. Last Orders is added to the kit as a move for slot 4: every ordinary enemy on screen is left with a sliver of health and stunned for 3 seconds.
6. **The stockroom** (`c7.stealth.stock`, stealth). Four `clerk`s are doing a stocktake after hours. They are precise and their patrols are perfectly regular, which is the weakness. Key clue: `c7.clue.permit` (a staff parking permit for "Gate C, service entrance"). Clean-run reward: a can of stout in the staff fridge with Gary's name on it. He got it shipped out of curiosity and does not see the appeal.
7. **The yard** (`c7.fight.yard`, fight). Three `mascot`s and three `clerk`s. The clerks line up to fire, and a line is what The Stumble and the umbrella are for.
8. **The van** (`c7.explore.van`). Key clue: `c7.clue.route` (a delivery log: Roswell to Nevada every Thursday, returning empty).
9. **Deduction board** (`c7.deduction.nevada`, deduction). Link `c7.clue.payslip`, `c7.clue.permit` and `c7.clue.route` to name the lead: Meridian Basin Facility, Gate C.
10. **The three mascots** (`c7.boss.mascots`, boss).

## Boss: three "mascots" who are not mascots

Zip, Zap and Zorp, in seven-foot foam costumes, on the strip at dusk. Tourists film it and assume it is a show.

- **Zip** tumbles: a rolling body slam along a marked line (heavy), then lies on his back for 2 seconds.
- **Zap** shoots: three-bolt fans from a foam ray gun that is not foam (ordinary each). The fans have gaps to walk through.
- **Zorp** shields: stands in front of the other two with a novelty sign that blocks everything from the front.
- They keep formation. The Stumble goes through the line and knocks the sign out of Zorp's hands for 3 seconds, and that is the intended answer. Going round the side works too, slowly. When one falls, the other two speed up. When the last falls, the tourists applaud, and one tips Andrew a dollar and hands him the prize: an inflatable alien off the rack.

Arena: 30 × 14 of the strip.

## Back to the bar

The bar is at the near end of the strip and the shop is at the far end. The stockroom's back gate opens onto the strip once the stockroom is passed.

## Closing scene

Outside the shop at night. Gary sees Andrew off, wishes him luck and asks not to be mentioned. Andrew now has a dollar. Myko says the roads here are narrow.
